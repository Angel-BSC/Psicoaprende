# Máquina de garra — propuesta visual v1

Dos mockups estáticos generados con la herramienta integrada de imágenes el 23/10/2026 y aprobados por el usuario. El exterior es el ancla visual del interior. Tras la petición «sigamos» se integró el exterior como acceso y se generaron piezas independientes para un prototipo interactivo. Los mockups originales se conservan aquí; sprites y prompts nuevos están en `assets/images/claw/README.md`, desde la raíz del proyecto. La pantalla interactiva adapta esta propuesta, no reproduce cada píxel del mockup.

- exterior.png: máquina completa, acceso al minijuego.
- interior.png: vista interior, flechas izquierda/derecha y botón AGARRAR.

Dirección: pixel art, verde salvia, lavanda, crema y ámbar; mantener las dos pantallas coherentes. La tipografía real se implementará como texto accesible, no como etiquetas incrustadas en una imagen interactiva. Para producción se debe preparar una cuadrícula de píxel consistente y separar fondo, marco/cristal, carro, cable, garra abierta/cerrada, cápsulas y controles. Las propuestas no garantizan recorte directo ni consistencia perfecta de sprites. La abertura de entrega debe quedar en una posición alcanzable por la garra; verificar su geometría al pasar a la escena 2D.

Interacción aprobada por el usuario: movimiento manual e interactuable. Propuesta: mantener flechas para mover; pulsar AGARRAR inicia descenso, cierre, recogida y entrega animada; tocar cápsula abre pregunta. Agarre tolerante propuesto, todavía por validar. No hay motor físico, animación, recompensas ni tienda implementados en esta etapa.

## Prompt exterior (herramienta integrada)

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished portrait mobile game screen concept for PsicoAprende, a psychology study app for university students. This is proposal 1: EXTERIOR of an original capsule claw machine, an appealing calm pixel-art study arcade.
Portrait composition approximately 2:3. Full screen artwork, NO phone hardware, NO multi-panel board. Warm ivory background #F8F7F3 with generous breathing room. Top small heading exactly "PSICOAPRENDE"; below title exactly "Cápsulas de estudio". Center one tall beautiful claw-machine cabinet, full machine visible with feet, frontal view with a very subtle visible right side. Cabinet cream enamel, muted sage green #426B5D frame, pale lavender panels #D9CDE5, dark green outlines #273C34, restrained warm amber #F2B85B indicator lights. Top marquee has a small original pixel brain symbol, not a corporate logo. Large glass chamber with pale mint back wall, visible horizontal rail and centered suspended cable and open three-prong mechanical claw. Bottom of glass contains a small pile of about 12 chunky rounded two-piece capsules, sage/lavender/cream with a few amber ones, each with a clear seam and small square highlight. Lower cabinet has two left/right arrow buttons, one round amber grab button, and a centered collection hatch. The controls should look like the same machine that can later be zoomed into. Pixel-stepped bevels and a compact contact shadow beneath feet. Below machine one wide pixel-edged sage button with text exactly "Toca para entrar". Small supporting caption exactly "Una cápsula, una pregunta".
ART DIRECTION: authentic crisp hand-placed 16-bit pixel art with a consistent visible coarse pixel grid, flat clusters of limited colors, stepped outlines, sparse purposeful dithering, no smoothed diagonal curves, no glossy 3D render, no watercolor or grain. Game art and buttons must all use the same pixel scale and design vocabulary. Text clearly readable with careful typography. Friendly, soft academic atmosphere, refined not childish; avoid casino neon. Enough contrast to distinguish claw from glass. Original design, no stock watermark, no borrowed characters, no copyrighted game logos, no skull symbols, no coin prices, no invented progress stats. This is a visual mockup, not a sprite sheet, not an animation.

## Prompt interior (herramienta integrada)

Referencia: exterior.png, usada como guía de identidad y estilo, no como objetivo a sobrescribir.

Use case: ui-mockup.
Input image 1: STYLE AND MACHINE IDENTITY REFERENCE ONLY, the exterior screen of our original PsicoAprende capsule claw machine. Generate a NEW companion portrait mobile screen, showing its INTERIOR GAMEPLAY VIEW. Do not reproduce the full exterior cabinet, do not make a before-after board.
Must be clearly the same machine: sage green frame, cream enamel, lavender control panel, amber central button, mint glass/back wall, very subtle botanical motifs, dark green outlines, silver mechanical claw with sage trolley, identical two-piece cream/sage/lavender/amber capsules with equatorial seams. Match the reference's crisp chunky pixel art and shading, not smooth vector or 3D. Original pixel-art graphics throughout with one consistent pixel scale.
Composition approximately 2:3 vertical phone screen, no phone hardware. Top minimal ivory header with a left back arrow and heading exactly "Elige tu cápsula". Beneath it a large front-facing close interior view occupying most of the screen. Slim sage frame around glass. Horizontal mechanical rail across the TOP of chamber, sage trolley slightly left of center, a vertical ribbed cable down to one prominent silver open claw, facing viewer, with three believable prongs including a small rear prong. Large clear open travel area around claw so manual left/right movement is understandable. Bottom third of chamber: about 12 large overlapping round capsules identical to reference, not emoji faces, not skulls; use a mix of sage, lavender, ivory and warm amber. Keep capsule silhouettes readable and their seams clear. Muted mint backdrop, soft pixel glass reflections only at edges; no intense lighting or blur. Small collection hatch visible near lower chamber edge without obstructing capsules.
Bottom control console in lavender and cream: three well spaced large touch controls matching exterior. Left square button with a LEFT arrow, central prominent round amber button, right square button with a RIGHT arrow. Directly underneath central button write exactly "AGARRAR". Buttons should appear pressable, with stepped pixel bevels. Under console, on ivory area write exactly "Mantén las flechas para mover" and on next line "Pulsa AGARRAR para bajar". Readable dark green type; no other copy, no stats, no coin costs, no question overlay yet. Keep entire controls and header inside safe margins, no cropped text.
This is a polished preview of a manual interactive claw-machine screen, but the output itself is a STATIC design proposal. Maintain the relaxed academic/cozy pixel-art identity of image 1, no realistic render, no glossy 3D, no new mascots, no borrowed trademarks, no watermark, no extra decorative clutter.
